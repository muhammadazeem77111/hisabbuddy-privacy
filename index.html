HisabBuddy — Privacy Policy

App: HisabBuddy – Expense & Loan (com.aistudio.expensetrack.pkrhqa) Developer: Muhammad Azeem Contact: muhammad.azeem7711@gmail.com Last updated: 7 November 2026

HisabBuddy is a personal money notebook: it helps you record your income, expenses, budgets, committees (BC) and loans. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the app keeps, where it is kept, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the email above.

The short version

  • Your records live on your phone. HisabBuddy works fully offline and without any account.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app contains no ads and no analytics or crash-tracking tools.
  • The only time your records can leave your phone is when you choose to: (a) turn on the optional Google cloud backup, or (b) export/share a file yourself (PDF, CSV or a backup file).
  • HisabBuddy never moves real money. It does not connect to any bank. It is a record-keeping book only.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you type into HisabBuddy is stored in the app's own database on your device, including:

  • Income entries (amount, source name, date, and whether the money was received in cash or in a bank account)
  • Expense entries (amount, category, date, who paid, cash or account)
  • Transfers between your own cash and account pockets
  • The names of income sources and of people ("payers") that you create
  • Monthly budgets
  • Committee (BC) records: committee name, instalment amounts, dates paid, and the turn/receipt record
  • Loan records: the name you enter for a person, amounts given or taken, repayments, dates and notes
  • Your settings, such as language (English/Urdu), theme, and reminder preferences
  • If you turn on App Lock: a salted cryptographic hash of your PIN — never the PIN itself — so the app can check the PIN you type without storing it. Fingerprint unlock, if you enable it, uses your phone's own fingerprint system; the app never receives or stores your fingerprint.

This information stays on your device unless you use one of the optional features in sections 2 and 3. Uninstalling the app removes the app's data from the device, except for any copy you exported or backed up yourself.

2. Optional Google sign-in and cloud backup

Signing in is optional. The app works completely without it.

If you choose to sign in with Google (in Profile):

  • Sign-in is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication (a Google service). The app receives your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account and shows them in the app so you can see which account is connected.
  • Cloud backup: when you tap "Back up now" — or if you turn on the optional auto-backup switch (it is off by default) — the app uploads one backup file: an exact copy of the backup file you can also export yourself, containing the records listed in section 1. It is stored in Firebase Storage (a Google service) in a private folder tied to your account (users/<your-account-id>/), so only your signed-in account can read it. Auto-backup, when switched on, runs only while you are signed in and only after your data has changed.
  • You can restore that backup onto a phone where you are signed in with the same Google account.
  • You can delete your cloud backup and this app's account at any time from inside the app (Profile → Delete cloud backup & account). This permanently deletes the backup file stored with Google and removes the app's account for you. It does not delete the records on your phone.
  • You can sign out at any time; your on-device records are unaffected.

Google processes the sign-in and storage services under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Exports and sharing (always started by you)

HisabBuddy can create files for you — PDF reports, a CSV export of your records, and a backup file of your whole data. These files are created only when you tap the export option, and they go only where you send them through Android's share sheet or file picker (for example, to your own WhatsApp, email, or Drive). The app does not upload these files anywhere by itself.

The app also lets you share a loan reminder message through your own messaging apps. The message is written by the app from the loan details you recorded, but you choose the app and the person, and you press send.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders generated on your own phone — for example, a loan that is due, a committee instalment date, or a budget alert. No notification content is sent from any server.

5. Android system backup

HisabBuddy allows Android's built-in backup feature. If you have turned on device backup in your phone's settings (under your Google account), Android itself may include this app's data in that system backup. That backup is controlled by you and Google in your device settings, not by this app's cloud backup feature.

6. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not show ads, and we include no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or messages.
  • We do not ask for your bank login, card numbers, or any payment credentials, and the app cannot make payments or transfers of real money.
  • Apart from the optional Google sign-in and cloud backup described in section 2, the app sends no data to us or to any third party.

7. Your controls

At any time, from inside the app, you can:

  • Delete individual records (income, expenses, loans, committees) and edit or rename your sources and people.
  • Clear all data (Profile → Clear All Data): permanently erases every record stored on this device by the app.
  • Sign out of the optional Google account.
  • Delete your cloud backup and account (section 2).
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in your phone's settings.

If you delete the app's account using the in-app option, the cloud backup and the account are removed from Google's services for this app. Copies you exported yourself (PDF/CSV/backup files) remain wherever you saved them, under your control.

8. Children

HisabBuddy is a general money record-keeping tool for adults managing their own finances. It is not designed for, or directed at, children, and it contains no content made for children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in an important way, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects how your data is handled, note it in the app's Play Store "What's new" text.
